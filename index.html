How to read the map

Four lenses prevent a flattened story

A single biography or timeline cannot explain the human-computing system. These lenses keep technical process and institutional power in the same frame.

01

Material

Follow the paper, instruments, calculators, desks, tapes, graphs, cards, and machines through which flight became measurable.

02

Social

See checking, mentoring, rides, housing, supervision, and friendship as infrastructure—not merely personal background.

03

Institutional

Trace how titles, salaries, segregation, work allocation, authorship, and advocacy distributed authority unevenly.

04

Archival

Ask which artifacts survived, which names reached reports, whose memories were recorded, and where the map must show uncertainty.

Continuity through automation

The cabinet did not replace the system. It inherited it.

Electronic mainframes accelerated arithmetic, but they arrived without aeronautical software, local operating knowledge, or automatic trust. The women who understood the manual workflow translated its formulas, checks, and expectations into code.
